WHO WE ARE

Built on Trust,
Driven by Results

Welcome to Surkhaab Consultants Private Limited, a trusted consulting and business solutions company committed to helping businesses grow with confidence and efficiency. Incorporated on 19 December 2022, we are a privately held, non-government company registered under the Registrar of Companies, Delhi.

We specialize in delivering professional business consulting and support services tailored to the unique needs of commercial clients across various industries. Our focus is on providing practical, reliable, and result-oriented solutions that help organizations streamline operations, improve performance, and achieve sustainable growth.

The company operates under NIC Code 7499 – "Other business activities n.e.c.", which reflects our broad consulting capabilities and commitment to serving diverse business sectors. Whether you are a startup, a growing enterprise, or an established organization, we are dedicated to being your reliable consulting partner.
